Talk About Your Home Loan

Homebuyer Resources

A little preparation goes a long way.

Use these starting points to organize your questions and prepare for a conversation about your mortgage.

Documents

Documents commonly requested

The exact documents depend on your circumstances and the loan program. Your loan professional will explain what is needed.

  • Employed / W-2 Borrowers

    Common requests may include identification, recent pay statements, W-2 forms and account statements for funds used in the transaction.

  • Self-Employed Borrowers

    Common requests may include identification, tax returns, business or income documentation and relevant account statements.

  • Retired / Fixed-Income Borrowers

    Common requests may include identification, pension or Social Security award information and relevant account statements.

These are general examples, not a complete application checklist. Additional documents may be requested.

Security note

Do not send identification, tax returns, bank statements or other sensitive documents through a general website inquiry. Use the designated document-submission process when instructed.

Terms

Mortgage terms in plain language

Down Payment
The portion of the purchase price paid upfront rather than financed through the mortgage.
Earnest Money
A deposit made as part of a purchase agreement. Its treatment depends on the contract and closing arrangements.
Appraisal
An assessment of property value used in the financing process. It serves a different purpose from a home inspection.
Mortgage Insurance
Insurance required with some loan arrangements. Its cost and cancellation rules depend on the loan type and applicable requirements.
Escrow
An arrangement used with some mortgages to collect funds for expenses such as property taxes and homeowners insurance.
Closing Costs
Transaction expenses associated with completing a purchase or refinance. Ask for an explanation of the costs relevant to your loan.

Questions worth asking

  • What costs and mortgage-insurance requirements apply?

  • What could affect the timeline?
  • What should I understand before accepting the loan terms?
