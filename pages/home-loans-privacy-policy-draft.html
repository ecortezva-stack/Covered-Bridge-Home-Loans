Privacy Policy

This draft identifies the privacy information to be confirmed for the Covered Bridge Home Loans website and its general inquiry process.

Draft — business identity and data practices require confirmation before publication.

1.Website operator

Review: Confirm the responsible legal entity, trading name and privacy contact. Do not replace a registered entity name solely because the website branding changes.

2.Information collected

The planned general inquiry form requests contact details and a brief description of the visitor’s home-loan questions.

Review: Confirm final form fields, logs and other collection methods.

3.How information is used

Proposed purposes include responding to inquiries and managing related communications.

Review: Confirm actual purposes and any separately authorized marketing activity.

4.Providers and sharing

Review: Identify relevant CRM, website and service providers and explain any sharing with mortgage/application providers.

5.Separate mortgage applications

Review: Identify the designated application provider and the policies/disclosures applicable to that separate process.

6.Cookies and analytics

Review: Confirm which technologies are actually enabled.

7.Retention and protection

Review: Confirm retention practices and accurately describe safeguards. Do not promise absolute security.

8.Requests and communication preferences

Review: Supply the correct contact channel and applicable procedures.

9.External links

External websites and application services may have their own privacy policies.

10.Effective date and contact

Review: Supply the approved effective date, responsible entity and contact information.
